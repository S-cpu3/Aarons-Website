01 / Open Source
SecureCycle
SecureCycle is a privacy and security focused mobile app designed for tracking menstrual cycles and storing reproductive health information for women. All data is processed locally on the app and can be protected by biometrics and App PIN.

02 / Open Source
StealthDetect
StealthDetect is a stalkerware detection app for mobile devices that uses network traffic analysis to detect indicators of compromise (IOCs). The app creates a local VPN to intercept DNS queries.
