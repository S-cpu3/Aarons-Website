About Me

I'm a cybersecurity researcher pursuing a Master's in Cybersecurity alongside my Bachelor's in Computer Science. My work focuses on protecting vulnerable populations from digital surveillance threats, particularly stalkerware and privacy-invasive technologies.

I have participated in many bug bounty programs where I have been recognized by multiple Hacker Hall of Fames.

I'm passionate about responsible disclosure, privacy rights, and developing practical security solutions for those who need them most.

Open source tool by the Electronic Frontier Foundation that detects cellular-level spying. Runs on an Orbic mobile hotspot and analyzes network traffic in real-time to identify suspicious events, including unusual requests like cell tower downgrade attempts to 2G networks.
